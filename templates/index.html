Deep Learning in Health Care

The Deep Learning (DL) technologies becoming ever present in modern business and everyday life is also steadily being applied to health care. The use of Deep Learning in health care has the potential to assist health care providers in many aspects of patient care and administrative processes. Most DL and health care technologies have strong relevance to the health care field, but the tactics they support can vary significantly. And while some articles on Deep Learning in health care suggest that the use of Deep Learning in health care can perform just as well or better than humans at certain procedures, such as diagnosing disease, it will be a significant number of years before DL in health care replaces humans for a broad range of medical tasks.

Machine Learning In Health Care

Machine learning is one of the most common forms of Artifical intelligence in health care. It is a broad technique at the core of many approaches to AI and health care technology and there are many versions of it. Using Artifical intelligence in health care, the most widespread utilization of traditional machine learning is precision medicine. Being able to predict what treatment procedures are likely to be successful with patients based on their make-up and the treatment framework is a huge leap forward for many health care organizations. The majority of AI in health care that uses machine learning and precision medicine applications require data for training, for which the end result is known. This is known as supervised learning.
